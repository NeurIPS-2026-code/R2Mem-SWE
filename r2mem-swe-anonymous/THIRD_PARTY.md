# Third-party dependencies

This archive contains the R²-Mem adapter and supporting scripts, not vendored upstream repositories, models, datasets, or images. Upstream names and URLs below identify public dependencies, not the submission authors.

- mini-SWE-agent: https://github.com/SWE-agent/mini-swe-agent , pinned commit `04d809ceab9df28f9adaed044884180159172930`. Its prompts, tool parser and execution loop are used through the dependency.
- SWE-bench: https://github.com/SWE-bench/SWE-bench , harness commit `53448dd9e6c8868e6349b06dca29da80ae6b7187`. Official patch application and grading remain in this dependency.
- SWE-bench Verified dataset: `princeton-nlp/SWE-bench_Verified`, revision `c104f840cc67f8b6eec6f759ebc8b2693d585d4a`.
- Sentence Transformers, Transformers, PyTorch, LiteLLM and OpenAI Python SDK are external dependencies with their respective licenses.

Obtain these resources separately and retain their upstream license notices. No new blanket license over third-party material is asserted by this archive.
